Legal and privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information WorkMood collects, how it is used, and the choices available to workers and hirers using the platform.

Effective date: April 4, 2026

1. Introduction

WorkMood connects workers and clients for on-demand services. By using the platform, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Name, phone number, age, profile photo, and address details.
  • Real-time GPS location used for nearby job matching.
  • Optional government-issued identification for verification.
  • Transaction details related to platform payments.

3. Why We Collect It

  • Create and manage accounts.
  • Support OTP-based authentication.
  • Match workers with nearby job opportunities.
  • Help prevent fraud and misuse.
  • Maintain payment and transaction records.

4. Legal Basis

  • User consent.
  • Legitimate interests such as safety and fraud prevention.
  • Legal or regulatory obligations.

5. Authentication and Payments

Secure access is handled through OTP-based login.

Payments may be processed through Razorpay or a similar payment partner.

  • WorkMood does not store card or bank details directly.
  • Payment partners handle sensitive payment information securely.
  • Refunds, if any, are subject to applicable law and platform policy.

6. Data Sharing

WorkMood does not sell personal data.

  • Relevant information may be shared between users to enable work coordination.
  • Trusted service providers may process data on our behalf.
  • Information may be disclosed if required by law.

7. Security and Retention

We use reasonable safeguards such as HTTPS and controlled access, but no system can guarantee complete security.

Data is retained while an account remains active or for as long as needed to meet legal and operational requirements.

8. User Rights

  • Access and update account information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.

Account deletion requests can be sent to workmood26@gmail.com. Deletion may take 7 to 30 days where legally permitted.

9. Age Restriction, Changes, and Contact

Users must be at least 18 years old to use the platform.

This policy may be updated from time to time, with changes communicated in-app or on the site.

For questions, contact workmood26@gmail.com.

By using WorkMood, you consent to this policy.
