Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Who can use WorkMood?

Workers looking for local daily, instant, or scheduled job opportunities across skilled and non-skilled categories.

How do payments work?

The app can support cash and online payment flows, while keeping the worker’s earnings breakdown visible and easy to understand.

Why is location important?

Location helps match workers with nearby jobs so travel is reduced and job acceptance becomes faster.

Can skilled and non-skilled workers both join?

Yes. The platform is designed to support a wide range of work categories, from helpers and cleaners to electricians and plumbers.
