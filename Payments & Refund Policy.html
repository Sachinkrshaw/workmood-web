Payments & Refund Policy

This policy explains how WorkMood handles platform feess, user-to-user payments, payment processing, refunds, and misuse.

Effective date: April 4, 2026

1. Overview

WorkMood is a platform that connects workers and hirers.

  • We charge a platform service fees, from workers after successful job connections.
  • We do not handle service payments between users.

2. Platform fees

  • Workers required to pay a platform fees after receiving work through WorkMood.
  • Platform fees is charged only for platform usage and connection services.

Important:

  • Platform fees is not a payment for employment.
  • WorkMood does not guarantee jobs or earnings.

3. Payments Between Users

Workers and hirers pay each other directly.

This may include:

  • Cash.
  • UPI or personal payment apps.
  • Any mutually agreed method.

WorkMood is not involved in these transactions.

4. platform fees Payment Responsibility

  • Workers are responsible for paying applicable platform feess after successful job engagement.
  • Failure to pay may result in account suspension, restricted access, or permanent ban.

5. Refund Policy

Platform feess, are generally non-refundable.

Refunds may be considered only in cases of:

  • Technical errors.
  • Duplicate charges.
  • Legal requirements.

6. Payment Processing

  • Platform payments are processed securely via third-party providers, such as Razorpay.
  • WorkMood does not store card or bank details.

7. Failed or Incorrect Payments

If you face a failed payment or incorrect charge, contact:

workmood26@gmail.com

Valid issues will be reviewed and resolved.

8. Fraud & Misuse

Any misuse of the payment system may result in account suspension or permanent ban.

9. Important Disclaimer

  • WorkMood does not guarantee jobs or income.
  • Platform feess apply only for successful connections.

10. Changes to Policy

We may update this policy at any time. Continued use means acceptance.
