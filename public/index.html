Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Who can use WorkMood?

Workers looking for local daily, instant, work opportunities across skilled and non-skilled categories.

How do payments work?

WorkMood does not process payments between hirers and workers. Workers and hirers can decide payment terms independently. WorkMood only charges a small platform fee ₹20 per work from workers to continue receiving work opportunities.

Why is location important?

Location helps match workers with nearby works so travel is reduced and work acceptance becomes faster.

Can skilled and non-skilled workers both join?

Yes. The platform is designed to support a wide range of work categories, from helpers and cleaners to electricians and plumbers.
