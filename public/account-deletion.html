Account Deletion Request

To request deletion of your WorkMood account, email workmood26@gmail.com. For security, include the registered phone number linked to the account you want removed.

  • Use the subject line "WorkMood Account Deletion Request" for faster routing.
  • Requests may be verified before deletion begins.
2

How Users Can Delete Their Account

  • Email workmood26@gmail.com from any contact address you prefer.
  • Mention your registered phone number and any account identification details that help us locate the profile.
  • Wait for confirmation from WorkMood support while the request is reviewed and processed.
3

Data That Will Be Deleted

Once the deletion request is approved, WorkMood removes the account and the personal data associated with it from active systems, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate business obligations.

  • Profile details such as name, registered phone number, profile photo, and address information.
  • Account preferences, profile content, and app records no longer needed for legal or security purposes.
  • Authentication-related account records managed for WorkMood services.
4

Data That May Be Retained

Some records may need to be retained for legal, fraud prevention, tax, payment, audit, or compliance purposes even after an account deletion request is completed.

  • Transaction or billing records connected to platform fees or financial reporting.
  • Logs and records needed to investigate abuse, fraud, safety incidents, or policy violations.
  • Information retained to satisfy legal obligations, dispute handling, or regulatory requirements.
5

Processing Time

Account and personal data are generally deleted within 7-30 working days after the request is verified.

  • Processing may take longer if there are active disputes, unresolved support cases, or ongoing transactions.
  • Refund reviews, payment reconciliation, or compliance checks can also delay final deletion.
6

Contact Support

If you need help with an account deletion request or want an update on an existing request, contact WorkMood support directly by email.

  • Email: workmood26@gmail.com
  • Please include your registered phone number in every deletion-related email.
7

Secure Services Used by WorkMood

WorkMood uses Firebase Authentication to support secure sign-in and identity verification flows, and Cashfree for secure payment-related processing where applicable.

  • These service providers help WorkMood deliver account access and payment support securely.
  • WorkMood does not state that it stores full card or bank details when payment processing is handled by Cashfree.
