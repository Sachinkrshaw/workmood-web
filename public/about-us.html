Meet the Founder

WorkMood was founded by Sachin Kumar Shaw, a technology enthusiast and entrepreneur passionate about solving real-world problems through digital solutions.

The idea for WorkMood came from observing the challenges faced by both workers and hirers in local service markets. While many customers struggled to find available workers when needed, many workers were simultaneously missing opportunities because of location barriers and inefficient communication channels.

Recognizing this gap, Sachin started WorkMood with the goal of creating a platform that makes local work opportunities easier to discover and easier to access. Today, WorkMood continues to focus on building technology that supports local workers, empowers communities, and improves the way people connect for work.

About WorkMood

Connecting Local Workers and Hirers, Faster

WorkMood was created to solve a problem that exists in many local communities. People often need workers for household services, repairs, maintenance, delivery, and other tasks, but finding available and reliable workers nearby can be difficult. At the same time, many skilled workers struggle to find consistent opportunities because available jobs are often located far from where they live.

We also observed that workers and hirers frequently depend on brokers or intermediaries to connect with each other, which can increase costs, reduce transparency, and limit direct communication. WorkMood was built to make local hiring simpler, faster, and more accessible for everyone.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help local workers and hirers connect more efficiently through technology. We aim to create a platform where workers can discover opportunities closer to home, while hirers can quickly find suitable local workers based on their needs.

What We Do

WorkMood is a workforce discovery platform that helps connect workers and hirers within local communities. The platform allows workers to create profiles, showcase their skills, and receive work opportunities, while hirers can discover and contact workers based on location and service requirements.

Our Vision

We believe finding local work opportunities should be simple, transparent, and accessible. Our vision is to strengthen local employment networks by helping workers find more opportunities and enabling hirers to access local talent more easily.
