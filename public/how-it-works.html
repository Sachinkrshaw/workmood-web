Payments & Platform Model

How payments work on WorkMood

WorkMood helps workers and hirers connect, while keeping platform access and work payments clearly separated.

Platform Role

WorkMood connects workers with hirers for work opportunities. The platform does not handle payments between hirers and workers.

Worker Platform Fee

Workers pay a small platform fee (₹20) after completing a work to continue receiving new work opportunities.

No Direct Payment Handling

Payments between hirers and workers happen outside the platform. WorkMood only charges for platform access.

WorkMood does not act as a payment intermediary or marketplace for financial transactions.
